# IRIS Playbook

**IRIS is the RSGx AI Hub assistant.** Ask it in plain English and it does the AI Hub work for you: share an
idea, track it, find the right AI tool, find a company document, or get help with your Claude seat.

This playbook is for RSGx staff. It explains what IRIS is for, what to ask, and what you get back.

---

## Using Claude? You're already set up

IRIS is switched on in Claude for everyone at RSGx.

1. Open Claude (claude.ai or the Claude desktop app) and sign in with your RSGx Microsoft account.
2. Ask your question. Mention IRIS if you like, for example: *"Ask IRIS what AI tools we have for safety."*
3. The first time, Claude asks you to connect IRIS and sign in. Approve it once and you're done.

IRIS always acts **as you**. It can only see and do what you can already see and do on the AI Hub, and everything
it does is recorded.

## What can I ask?

| You want to… | Ask something like… | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Share an idea | "Submit my idea: an AI tool that summarises daily site diaries" | Your idea is in the Opportunity Funnel, under your name, ready for review |
| Track your ideas | "Where are my ideas up to?" | Each of your ideas and the stage it has reached |
| Find an AI tool | "What AI apps do we have for safety?" | Approved apps on the AI Stack, with a short summary of each |
| Find a document | "Find the travel policy on the intranet" | The key points, with a link to the source page |
| Sort out your Claude seat | "What is my Claude seat status?" | Where your request is up to and what happens next |
| Learn | "What AI courses should I start with?" | Featured learning links from the AI team |
| Register something you built | "Register my dashboard on the AI Hub" | Your app enters the review path to go live for everyone |

More examples, including what managers can ask: [use-cases.md](use-cases.md).

## Claude seat questions

Email **ai@rsgx.com** to:
- give a seat back when you no longer need Claude
- remove the seat of a team member who has left
- tell us your functional manager has changed
- get help if you were approved but still can't sign in
- ask about usage limits

New to Claude? Request a seat on the AI Hub at https://ai.rsgx.com after completing the two required courses.
