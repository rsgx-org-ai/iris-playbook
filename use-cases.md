# IRIS use cases

Each use case shows what to ask and what you can expect back. Answers depend on your own AI Hub access.

## For everyone

### 1. Turn an idea into a proposal
- **Ask:** "Submit my idea: an AI tool that drafts RFI responses from past answers."
- **You get:** the idea logged in the Opportunity Funnel under your name. It is assessed and then reviewed by the
  AI Steer Co.
- **Why it matters:** good ideas don't get lost in inboxes, and every idea is judged the same way.

### 2. Follow your idea through
- **Ask:** "Where are my ideas up to?"
- **You get:** each of your ideas and its current stage, from Submitted to Deployed.
- **Why it matters:** no chasing, no guessing.

### 3. Find the right AI tool before building one
- **Ask:** "Is there already an AI tool for site inductions?" or "What AI apps do we have for safety?"
- **You get:** matching apps from the AI Stack, with a one-page summary of any app you pick.
- **Why it matters:** reuse what exists instead of paying for the same thing twice.

### 4. Find company information fast
- **Ask:** "Find the leave policy on the intranet and summarise it."
- **You get:** the key points and a link to the source page on the RSGx intranet.
- **Why it matters:** minutes, not a search through SharePoint folders.

### 5. Get unstuck with Claude
- **Ask:** "What is my Claude seat status?" or "How do I connect my files to Claude?"
- **You get:** a personal answer about your own request, or a how-to from the AI Hub help guide.

### 6. Keep learning
- **Ask:** "What's new on the AI Hub?" or "Show me Claude skills for writing reports."
- **You get:** the latest AI Hub news, ready-made Claude skills and featured learning links.

### 7. Get your own app shared with the business
- **Ask:** "I built a dashboard with Claude. How do I get it on the AI Stack?"
- **You get:** your app registered for review. See [What to expect when you deploy an app](#what-to-expect-when-you-deploy-an-app).

## For managers and leaders

### 8. See the whole portfolio
- **Ask:** "Which ideas are in Build or Test right now?"
- **You get:** the full Opportunity Funnel by stage.

### 9. Understand adoption and sentiment
- **Ask:** "How is Claude being used across functional groups?" or "What did the latest AI Pulse survey say?"
- **You get:** adoption by group and survey results. Small groups are never shown, to protect privacy.

### 10. Stay on strategy
- **Ask:** "Summarise the AI Strategy and where my area fits."
- **You get:** the current AI Strategy, on demand.

## What to expect when you deploy an app

Built a dashboard or tool with Claude and want colleagues to use it? Here is the path.

1. **Register it** on the AI Hub (Developer track), or ask IRIS to do it.
2. **Review.** The AI Hub assesses its value, effort and risks, and runs safety checks.
3. **Decision.** The AI Steer Co decides whether it goes ahead.
4. **Made company-owned.** Approved apps move into RSGx's own systems, so they don't depend on one person.
5. **Build, test, go live** with support from the AI team.
6. **Shared.** Live apps are listed on the AI Stack so everyone can find them.

**The safety checks, in plain English**
- No passwords or access keys are left in the app
- Settings files that could hold passwords are not included
- Third-party components are safe and up to date
- Licensing allows RSGx to use the app
- The app has a named business owner and technical owner

If a check finds something, you'll be told what to fix before it goes live. A password that was ever saved in an
app must be changed, not just deleted.
